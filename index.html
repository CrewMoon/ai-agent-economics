The economics of AI-agent delegation AI 智能体委托的经济学

When a machine agent executes the choice, who holds the right to delegate, what does the resulting income tell a government, and how should the work inside the agent be organized? 当决策由机器代理执行：委托权归谁，由此产生的收入还能告诉政府什么，代理内部的工作又该如何组织？

Research agenda 研究议程

AI agents increasingly act inside accounts, workflows, and labor decisions that used to be operated by people. Each of these substitutions breaks an assumption that standard economic tools quietly rely on: that the person choosing is the person whose welfare is at stake, and that an observed outcome carries a readable preference behind it.

AI 智能体正在接管过去由人操作的账户、工作流与劳动决策。每一次这样的替代，都会打破标准经济学工具默认的一个前提：做选择的人就是福利承担者，可观测的结果背后有一个可读的偏好。

Our four papers follow that break through four layers. Delegation Rights asks who should hold residual control over machine-mediated account use, and shows that platform control can suppress AI-complementary investment through hold-up. Welfare-Opaque Income takes delegation as given and asks what the tax base still reveals: when the execution rule is hidden, identical tax-base statistics can carry opposite welfare consequences, and the optimal-tax formula needs an extra execution wedge.

四份工作沿着这个断裂分四层推进。Delegation Rights 问的是：机器代理操作账户的剩余控制权该归谁；平台控制会通过套牢压制与 AI 互补的投资。Welfare-Opaque Income 接受委托这一既成事实，转而追问税基还能揭示什么：当执行规则不可观测，完全相同的税基统计量可以对应相反的福利含义，最优税公式因此需要补上一个执行楔子。

The other two open the agent itself. The Organization of Inference runs matched workflow experiments on externally verified software tasks and shows that the return to a larger inference budget depends on how the workflow is organized and what information reaches the planner. How Do Agent Harnesses Create Value? separates the harness into guidance and release control, and prices each against the liability assigned to a wrong acceptance.

另外两篇打开代理本身。The Organization of Inference 在外部可验证的软件任务上做配对工作流实验，证明更大的推理预算能换来多少产出，取决于工作流怎么组织、以及规划者拿到了什么信息。How Do Agent Harnesses Create Value? 把 harness 拆成「指导」与「放行控制」两部分，并按错误放行所承担的责任对两者分别定价。

The first two papers are mechanism and policy; the last two are controlled experimental evidence. Read together, they argue that agent-mediated economies need identification strategies, not just faster models.

前两篇是机制与政策，后两篇是受控实验证据。合起来的主张是：智能体中介的经济需要的是识别策略，而不只是更快的模型。

Delegation Rights: Property, Agency, and Investment Incentives in the Age of AI Agents

Yukun Zhang

Delegation is a distinct property right. Platform control creates hold-up and underinvestment; unconditional user control leaves security costs uninternalized. Certified delegation dominates both polar regimes. 委托是一项独立的产权。平台控制导致套牢与投资不足；无条件的用户控制则让安全成本无人内部化。认证式委托优于这两个极端。

Welfare-Opaque Income: Taxation under AI-Agent Delegation

Yukun Zhang · Kemu Xu · Yishen Chen

A hidden preference-to-execution mapping makes income welfare-opaque: the same tax-base response can carry different welfare consequences. Optimal tax needs a response-weighted execution wedge. 隐藏的「偏好—执行」映射让收入变得福利不透明：相同的税基反应可以对应不同的福利后果。最优税需要补上一个按反应加权的执行楔子。
